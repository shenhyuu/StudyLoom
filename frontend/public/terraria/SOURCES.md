## Design references

- [Inventory](https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Inventory): blue slots, item icons, selection border; shortcuts navigate the study room.
- [Guide](https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Guide), [Merchant](https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Merchant), [Dryad](https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Dryad): resident identities and roles. All study dialogue is original, not in-game quotation.
- [Glowing Mushroom biome](https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Glowing_Mushroom_biome): giant blue mushrooms and Truffle.
- [The Hallow](https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Hallow): colorful foliage and cyan grass.
- [Campfires](https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Campfires): warm light and Cozy Fire motif. Study-room focus is not an in-game health calculation.
- [Fallen Star](https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Fallen_Star): night-only collectible; one per biome per mounted view, a local theme Easter egg.
- [Music Boxes](https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Music_Boxes): music panel housing; the existing API track remains the displayed track.
- User references also included [Huiji Wiki](https://terraria.huijiwiki.com/wiki/首页) and [Fandom Wiki](https://terraria.fandom.com/wiki/Terraria_Wiki); homepages returned access errors during research.

The landscapes, tile patterns, player avatars and interface layout are authored SVG/CSS. Pylon travel, milestone titles and NPC dialogue are study-room adaptations, not a simulation of Terraria progression or NPC spawning.
